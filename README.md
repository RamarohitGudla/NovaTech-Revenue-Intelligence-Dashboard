# NovaTech Revenue Intelligence Dashboard

## 📊 

The **NovaTech Revenue Intelligence Dashboard** is a Business Intelligence project developed using **Amazon QuickSight**.

The project brings together **Marketing, Sales, and Customer Support data** to provide a unified view of business performance. The dashboard helps identify revenue opportunities, understand sales performance, evaluate marketing effectiveness, and monitor customer health.

The project was developed as part of the **AWS AI & ML Nanodegree program**.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Amazon QuickSight
- QuickSight Q
- AWS
- CSV Datasets
- Data Analysis
- Business Intelligence
- Data Visualization

---

## 📁 Project Data

The project uses three main datasets:

- **CRM Deals** – Sales opportunities, deal stages, deal values, sales regions, products, and loss reasons.
- **Marketing Campaigns** – Campaign channels, campaign spend, responses, and attributed revenue.
- **Support Tickets** – Customer support activity, ticket priority, product areas, resolution time, and customer sentiment.

The datasets are connected using **`account_id`** for customer-level analysis.

# 🤖 QuickSight Q

**QuickSight Q** was configured to enable natural-language questions about the business data.

The Topic was improved using:

- Field descriptions
- Business terminology
- Synonyms
- Custom instructions
- Multiple datasets

Questions were tested **before and after Topic configuration** to compare the quality and accuracy of QuickSight Q responses.

The results are documented in the **Q Exploration Log**.
